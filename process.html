How we build

A process built around site visits, not guesswork.

Six stages, each with a defined output — so you always know exactly what's been decided and what's next.

01

Consultation

We start with a call or in-person meeting to understand your plot, budget range, timeline and what you actually need the space to do — before any design work begins.

02

Site Visit & Assessment

Our site engineer visits the plot to check soil, access, orientation and any structural constraints that affect design and cost.

03

Design, Material Tier & Quote

You get a floor plan/layout, a choice of Basic, Standard or Premium material tier, and an itemised cost sheet — no lump-sum guessing.

04

Construction

Foundation, structure, brickwork and rough-in electrical/plumbing proceed under continuous site supervision, with milestone check-ins.

05

Finishing & Interiors

Plastering, painting, flooring, modular fittings and fixtures are completed and inspected against the agreed material tier.

06

Handover

A final walkthrough, documentation of work completed, and keys handed over — with the itemised cost sheet closed out against the original quote.
