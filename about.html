About SJ Infra

Built on-site, not on a sales script.

SJ Infra is a construction and interiors studio based in Gandhinagar, Ballari, working across ten towns in North Karnataka and the neighbouring Andhra Pradesh border.

Our story

A studio built around one accountable team.

Most construction in this region moves through a chain of separate contractors — one for the shell, another for plumbing, another for interiors — with no single party accountable for how it all fits together. SJ Infra runs the opposite way: one engineering-led team carries a project from the first site visit to the final coat of paint.

That structure is what makes itemised, transparent pricing possible — when one team owns the whole job, there's nowhere for costs to hide between contractors.
